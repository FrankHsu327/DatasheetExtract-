All are public datasheets: search the MPN on the manufacturer's website and save the PDF
into datasheet\ under the local file name below, so the scripts and golden files find it.

Notes
- The golden files were hand-transcribed from these exact documents; a different revision may
  move tables/figures to other pages and lower the scores.
- samples\DS325Q32.pdf is a synthetic test PDF; tests regenerate it with scripts\make_sample.py.
